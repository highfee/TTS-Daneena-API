What happens as these numbers drop?

1. The L1 Spec Loss (The Clarity Gate)

Right now, at 0.599, the Mel-spectrogram the model creates is a bit "blurry."
When your 300k HiFi-GAN receives a blurry spectrogram, it tries to fill in the
gaps, which can cause that "metallic hiss."

  - At 0.450: The spectrogram becomes sharp. HiFi-GAN will have a much easier
    time producing crystal-clear high frequencies (the "s" and "t" sounds).

2. The Pitch Loss (The Robot Killer)

At 0.335, your model is likely predicting a "mean pitch." It knows the speaker's
voice is generally deep or high, but it isn't predicting the inflection.

  - The Problem: Humans constantly change pitch. Robots stay on one note.
  - The Target (0.200): When this drops, the model starts predicting the
    "prosody"—the way a person’s voice rises when they are surprised or drops
    when they are sad. This is the most important metric for your goal.

3. Grad Norm & Learning Rate

Your optim0_lr0=7.570e-05 is good. It shows the warmuplr scheduler is working.

  - Watch this: As you approach Epoch 400-500, the learning rate will start to
    decay (get smaller). This is when the model does its "fine-polishing." If
    you stop before the decay happens, you miss out on the most realistic
    details.
